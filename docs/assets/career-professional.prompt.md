# JobMatch home banner asset

- Asset: `frontend/public/images/career-professional.png`
- Generator: built-in `image_gen` tool, through the `imagegen` skill.
- Output: 1024 × 1536 PNG with transparent alpha; generated for this project.
- The page crops the portrait at the waist using CSS. The decorative background is CSS, and the icons are SVG.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: transparent cutout portrait for the hero banner of a Vietnamese recruitment website.
Primary request: A polished professional photo of a smiling young adult Vietnamese woman with long dark brown gently wavy hair, wearing a crisp white collared blouse with sleeves rolled to the forearms and dark trousers, holding a slim dark gray tablet vertically against her waist with both hands. Her torso faces the camera, her head and eyes turn slightly toward the viewer's left with a warm confident smile.
Scene/backdrop: fully transparent background, clean alpha cutout, no environment, no ground shadow.
Composition/framing: portrait 2:3, waist-up from the top of the hair to just below the hips, all hair and both arms and hands fully inside the frame, slim subject centered and filling almost all image height, tiny top margin. Photo photographed at eye level.
Lighting/mood: soft bright studio daylight, natural skin texture, professional and approachable. Realistic photographic detail.
Constraints: only one woman and tablet. No text, no watermark, no extra objects, no shapes, no background, no white rectangle. Preserve true transparent alpha around the subject.
```
